# Keel

Keel is a subscription and recurring-payment tracker that answers the
question existing apps gloss over: not just *what* you pay, but *when* it
hits *which* account. It treats "buy now pay later" purchases as
first-class trackable plans, lets you assign every payment to a specific
bank account or card, and smooths irregular annual costs (school fees,
yearly licences) into your monthly budget — so cash-flow surprises stop
happening.

Currently in early development — iOS first, Android and web to follow.
